Quizlet Gravity privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Quizlet Gravity Android and iOS apps and their use of the game at quizletgravity.com. The operator is Marko Sarafijanovic. For privacy or support enquiries, contact quizletgravity@gmail.com.

Your study material

The game stores imported cards and preferences in local website storage inside the app. Pasted text and files you select are processed by the game on your device. The file picker grants access to the file you choose, not general access to your files. There is no Quizlet Gravity account or cross-device account sync.

If you import a public Quizlet set, its link is sent to our server, which uses Firecrawl to retrieve the public set. The server returns its terms and definitions to the app. The importer caches results with a one-hour revalidation interval. This is not a promise that all service-provider copies are erased after an hour. Do not include confidential information in a public set.

Connection, analytics and performance information

The game is hosted by Vercel. Network requests necessarily transmit technical information, including IP address, requested URL and browser/device information. The import endpoint uses the caller's IP address for short-lived rate limiting to prevent abuse. Import error logs record the set identifier and error category, rather than the full card text.

The website includes Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. They measure page use and performance. Vercel describes the analytics data as aggregated and not linked to a named person. It may include page URLs, referrers, approximate location, device/browser information and performance measurements. Analytics uses a request-derived visitor hash rather than third-party cookies, with its visitor session discarded after 24 hours. This does not mean that all aggregate measurements or hosting logs are deleted after 24 hours.

We do not add advertising tracking, sell study material, or request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone or camera. The apps do not contain Firecrawl credentials. Their internet connections use HTTPS.

Service providers and retention

Vercel provides hosting, caching, analytics and performance measurement. Firecrawl retrieves public pages at your request. These providers may process data outside your country. Provider-side storage and retention are governed by their services and policies. We do not claim that all app-related processing happens solely on your device.

Your choices and requests

You can use pasted text or a local file instead of importing a public set. To remove locally stored app data, clear the app's storage on Android, or delete the app on iOS. This removes local cards and settings rather than deleting the source Quizlet set. Device backups, if enabled by your platform, are managed through your device settings.

For questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion of data we hold, email quizletgravity@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or private study files. We may need enough information to locate the relevant request. There is no app account to delete.

This policy may change when the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
