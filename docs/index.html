Quizlet Gravity support

Turn study terms into a typing game. Import a public Quizlet set, paste terms, or choose a CSV/TSV text file, check your cards and play.

For help, email quizletgravity@gmail.com. Please include your device model, app version and what happened. Do not include passwords or private study material.

Common questions

Does the app work offline? An internet connection is required to load the game and import public sets.

Why can’t a set be imported? Check that the set is public. If the source cannot be read, paste your terms or import a local text file instead. Empty-sided cards are skipped and their count is reported.

Where are my cards saved? In the app’s local website storage. App storage is separate from your normal browser and is not synchronised between devices.
