Name: Quizlet Gravity

Google Play short description (80 characters maximum):
Turn your flashcards into a fast-paced typing game.

App Store subtitle (30 characters maximum):
Flashcards meet a typing game

Description:

Give your study terms a new challenge with Quizlet Gravity. Match falling prompts by typing the correct answer before they reach the bottom.

Bring your own learning material:
• Import a public Quizlet set by pasting its link, including links copied from the Quizlet sharing menu.
• Paste terms and definitions, or choose a CSV/TSV text file.
• Review and edit your cards before playing.
• Practise vocabulary, facts and other question-and-answer pairs.

Imported cards and preferences are kept in the app's local website storage. No Quizlet Gravity account is required. An internet connection is needed to load the game and import public sets. Import availability depends on the source set being public and accessible. If an import cannot be read, you can paste your terms or select a local file instead.

Quizlet Gravity is an independent project and is not an official Quizlet app.
